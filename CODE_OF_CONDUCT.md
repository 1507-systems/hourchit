# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith and arguing about the
code, not about you.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep criticism about the work: the design, the diff, the tradeoff.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. "No" usually means it doesn't
  fit the project's scope, not that the idea was bad.
- Assume ignorance before malice. Most friction is a missing piece of context.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised remarks, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private information
without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
else someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/1507-systems/hourchit/security/advisories/new),
which is the available private channel on this repository.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, or block
accounts. Given the size of the project, expect judgement rather than process.
Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared.
